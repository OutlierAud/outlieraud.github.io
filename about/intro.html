Odd. Aud. Outlier Aud

I believe every dataset holds a story, and some stories can change the world.

Hi! My name is Audrey. Some people call me Aud. Or Odd. I pretend I know which one they mean, but deep down, I suspect they're not sure either.

Coming to data science from creative multimedia, I want to be clear: I’m here to do the science, not just the storytelling around it. I’m studying statistics and programming in earnest, with every intention of continuing to learn throughout my career because an analysis deserves to be as visually clear as it is scientifically sound.

My background in communication and project management brings a complementary perspective, but technical rigor comes first. With a particular interest in environmental and social issues, I hope to bring both rigor and reach to the problems I take on.

This Is My Sandbox

This is where I explore ideas, run sometimes questionable experiments, break things (occasionally on purpose), and figure out what the data has to say. Environmental and social issues are recurring themes, but curiosity sometimes takes the front seat.

I’m particularly interested in civic technology, spatial analysis, and public policy: how our surroundings shape our experiences, how public resources are distributed, and what data can reveal about the social and environmental challenges facing our communities. I’m curious about how these insights can inform better decisions, improve public services, and make a tangible difference in people’s lives.

Drawing on my professional background, I approach data science as both analysis and communication. Sound analysis comes first, but I believe data becomes most powerful when people can connect with it emotionally and visually. Done well, complex data can become more than numbers and charts; it can help us see familiar problems differently, ask better questions, and discover new possibilities.

The learning never stops, and this portfolio is part of that process: a place to put what I learn into practice, follow my curiosity, and explore how I can contribute to work that matters in the real world.
