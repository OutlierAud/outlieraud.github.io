The maps show where raw counts of recorded shootings accumulated over 19 years.

What the counts leave out

❌ Exposure
To compare rates, I’d need to know how many people live in or pass through each area.
❌ Changes over time
Mapping shorter periods would show whether the same areas stood out year after year.
❌ Coverage
Unreported incidents and records without coordinates fall outside the mapped counts.

Next Steps

Compare rates
Use resident population to calculate per-capita rates. Look for foot traffic data for the overnight comparison.
Check whether clusters persist
Map shorter periods using the same grid and colour scale to see where clusters persisted or shifted.
Check data gaps
Count records missing coordinates by borough and year to see where the maps may understate recorded incidents.
